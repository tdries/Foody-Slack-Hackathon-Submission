How to use Foody

Work hard, skip hangry.
No app to install · lives in Slack
Group food ordering · 6 simple steps

From “let's eat something” to a shared basket — entirely in your channel.

1

Kick it off

Type let's eat something in any channel. Foody greets you and remembers your delivery address.

2

Pick a vibe

Tap a cuisine category — pizza, kebab, sushi, healthy… or skip straight to the top spots.

3

Choose a spot

The top 3 restaurants near you, scored and ranked. One tap to open the menu.

4

React to add

The top 10 dishes land pre-reacted. Tap an emoji to add it to the shared basket — the total updates live for everyone.

5

Order

Hit 🛒 Order now. Foody builds the basket on takeaway.com with a live progress card.

6

Done

A clean receipt lands in the thread — items, totals and ETA. Everyone's fed.

Two handy commands

change address to … updates your delivery spot · reset starts the session over
