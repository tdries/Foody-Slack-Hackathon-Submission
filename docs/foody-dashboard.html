What is Foody?

Group food ordering that lives in your chat.

Foody is a friendly assistant that lives right inside Slack. Someone types “let's eat something,” and Foody runs the whole lunch order as a conversation: it suggests nearby restaurants, gathers everyone's picks through emoji reactions, keeps a running total for the team, and places one shared order. The food was never the hard part. The coordination was, and Foody takes care of it.

How it works

1🗣️

Say the word

Type “let's eat something” in any channel. Foody jumps in.

2📍

It remembers you

Tell Foody your address once. It's saved for next time, per person.

3🍽️

Browse together

Foody posts the top spots nearby and their best dishes, right in the thread.

4😋

React to add

Everyone taps an emoji to add a dish. The shared basket and total update live.

5✅

One tap to order

Hit order, and a tidy receipt lands right back in the channel.
