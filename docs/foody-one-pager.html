Someone types “let's eat something” — Foody takes the whole team from hungry to ordered.

No app to download, no link to chase, no “what does everyone want?” thread. Foody picks up right where your team already works, walks the channel through choosing a restaurant, and builds one shared basket from everyone's emoji reactions. One tap and you're at checkout on takeaway.com.
