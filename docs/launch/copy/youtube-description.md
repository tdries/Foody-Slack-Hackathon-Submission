🍴 Foody — Group food ordering, right inside Slack. Work hard, skip hangry.

Someone on your team types "let's eat something" — and Foody runs the entire lunch order as a conversation. No app to install, no links to chase, no spreadsheet. The whole team just taps emoji reactions to build one shared basket, and Foody places the order on Just Eat Takeaway.com.

The food was never the hard part. The coordination is the tax. Foody removes it.

How it works (this 60-second demo):
→ Someone says "let's eat something" — Foody greets the channel and already knows the address
→ One tap picks a cuisine; Foody posts the top-rated spots nearby
→ The team reacts with emojis — every reaction drops a dish into the same shared basket
→ One tap to order, and Foody builds the exact basket on Just Eat Takeaway.com
→ A clean receipt lands right back in the thread

Why it's different
• 🪄 The interface is just emoji reactions — zero learning curve
• 🧭 The basket is reconciled from the actual reactions on the message, so it self-heals and never desyncs
• ⚙️ Deterministic, no LLM in the runtime path — same input, same result, every time
• 🔌 Private by default — runs over Slack Socket Mode, no public URL, nothing leaves your workspace
